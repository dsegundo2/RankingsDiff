Settings proposal

One setting controls the whole flow

Make the roster panel and auto-placement behavior optional, without hiding the normal Draft/target tools.

Draft mode Choose which price field appears when you mark a player as yours.
Recommended model: use Option B for half-screen browsers, keep the shortlist above the full roster, and show all roster slots even when empty. Auto-placement should honor standard eligibility: RB/WR/TE can fill FLEX; any extra eligible player goes to Bench. The panel can be disabled in Settings for users who only want rankings.
